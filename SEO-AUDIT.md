# DeepGlow SEO Audit (1 October 2026)

## Summary

The website itself is now well optimised for local search.
**The two biggest problems are outside the code:**

1. **www.deepglow.in does not exist yet.** The domain doesn't resolve, so Google can't find the site at all.
2. **DeepGlow has no Google Business Profile.** For searches like "beauty parlour near me" or "beauty parlour in Lohegaon", Google mostly shows Maps listings, not websites.

## Research findings

| Finding | What it means |
|---|---|
| `www.deepglow.in` and `deepglow.in` don't resolve (no DNS record) | Register the domain (if not already yours) and point it at your host. Until then, SEO work has no effect. |
| A company called Loris Healthcare sells a "Deep Glow Skin Brightening Cream" | Competing for "Deep Glow skincare" or "DeepGlow cosmetics" searches against a product brand is hard and would attract the wrong visitors. |

## Keyword strategy

The list you supplied was written for an online skincare *product* store (serums, moisturisers, "buy online", "skincare store").
DeepGlow sells *services at home*. Using those keywords would bring people who want to buy a serum and then leave, which hurts rankings.
Google also penalises pages stuffed with keywords that don't match what the page offers.

**Targeted on the site now:**

- **Brand:** DeepGlow, Deep Glow, DeepGlow beauty, DeepGlow beauty parlour
- **Main:** beauty parlour at home Lohegaon, home beauty parlour Pune, beautician at home Lohegaon
- **Services:** facial at home Pune, waxing at home Pune, D-Tan facial at home, Vitamin C clean-up, O3 facial, head massage at home, eyebrow, upper lip
- **Local:** Lohegaon, Pune, Maharashtra (Lohegaon PIN codes are 411032 and 411047)

**Not used, on purpose:** skincare products, buy serum online, moisturiser, skincare store, Mumbai, discounts and offers.
These don't match the business, and DeepGlow doesn't serve Mumbai.
If DeepGlow ever sells products, those keywords become relevant then.

## What to do next, in priority order

1. **Get the domain live.** Register `deepglow.in` (if needed), publish the site (Netlify or GitHub Pages, see README), and turn on HTTPS.
2. **Create a Google Business Profile** at business.google.com. This is the single biggest step for local search.
   - Category: "Beauty salon" (main) plus "Waxing hair removal service" and "Facial spa".
   - Choose **service-area business** and hide your home address. Set the service area to Lohegaon, Pune.
   - Use exactly the same name, phone and website everywhere: *DeepGlow*, *+91 7709753948*, *https://www.deepglow.in*.
   - Add the six packages as Services with prices, and add real photos of your kit and setup.
3. **Collect real Google reviews.** After each appointment, send the client your review link on WhatsApp. Reviews strongly affect Maps ranking. They will also replace the sample testimonials on the site.
4. **List DeepGlow free on Justdial and Sulekha** (category "Beauty parlour" / "Beautician at home", Lohegaon), using the same name, phone and website.
5. **Add Google Search Console**, verify the domain, and submit `https://www.deepglow.in/sitemap.xml`.
6. **Post on Instagram** (e.g. `deepglow.pune`). Add the profile link to the website and the Google profile.
7. **Later, when there's content to write:** separate pages for key services (e.g. "Waxing at home in Lohegaon", "D-Tan facial at home in Pune") and a few short guides (e.g. "D-Tan facial vs clean-up"). Each new page can rank for its own search phrase.

## Things deliberately not done

- **No hidden or repeated keyword lists.** Google treats this as spam.
- **No meta `keywords` tag.** Google ignores it.
- **No fake ratings or review counts** in structured data. Google can penalise this.
- **No claims to serve areas or sell products** DeepGlow doesn't offer.

Keyword search volumes were not measured, because that needs a tool like Google Keyword Planner. After launch, Search Console will show the real searches people use to find DeepGlow.
